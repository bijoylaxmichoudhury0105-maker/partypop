Let's Make Emails Work!
You need a FREE EmailJS account. It takes 3 minutes and lets PartyPop send real emails to your friends. No coding needed! 🥳
1 Go to emailjs.com and click Sign Up Free
2 After signing in, go to Email Services → Add New Service → choose Gmail → connect your Gmail account (e.g. boochou0105@gmail.com)
3 Note down your Service ID (looks like service_xxxxxxx)
📝
Create Email Template
This step is the most important! Follow EXACTLY — the "To Email" field is where most people go wrong.
1 Go to Email Templates → Create New Template
📋 Your EmailJS Template Should Look Like This:
To Email {{to_email}} ✅
Subject {{subject}} ✅
Body {{message}} ✅
⚠️ The "To Email" box is a SEPARATE input at the top of the template — NOT inside the body text!
⚠️ Critical: The To Email field is a dedicated input box near the top of the template editor — look for it above the Subject line. Type {{to_email}} exactly in that box. This is the #1 reason emails fail!
2 Click Save → note your Template ID (e.g. template_xxxxxxx)
🔑
Get Your Public Key
Almost there! One last thing to grab from EmailJS.
1 In EmailJS dashboard, click your Account (top right) → Account Settings
2 Find the Public Key section — copy that key (looks like XXXXXXXXXXXXXXX)
⚙️
Paste Your Keys
Enter the three values from EmailJS. These are saved only in your browser — completely private!

🔒 Keys stored locally in your browser only. Never sent anywhere except EmailJS.
